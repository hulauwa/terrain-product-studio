about=Terrain Product Studio turns one elevation raster into a full set of publication-ready layers: elevation color relief, hillshades, slope, aspect, TRI/TPI, roughness, curvatures, spot elevations, smoothed contours and rivers, geomorphons, SPI/STI, landslide and multi-hazard indexes, hydrology, an all-in-one GeoPackage bundle, a ready-to-save .qgz project and an interactive 3D web viewer. The Assistant tab suggests smart defaults (contour interval, stream threshold, river width/depth, working CRS) from your DEM; cartography can be restyled onto existing canvas, QML and print layouts without re-running the pipeline. 20 color ramps in Light/Dark/Scientific, one-click industry presets, STL/OBJ 3D-print export and a run-history journal.
