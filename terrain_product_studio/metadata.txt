about=Terrain Product Studio turns one elevation raster into a consistent package of publication-ready cartographic and analytical terrain layers: elevation color relief, hillshades, slope, aspect, TRI, TPI, roughness, curvatures, spot elevations, smoothed contours and rivers, geomorphon landforms, SPI/STI, landslide hazard, a multi-hazard composite index, hydrology, an all-in-one GeoPackage bundle, a ready-to-save QGIS project (.qgz) and an interactive 3D web viewer. A smart setup assistant suggests contour intervals and themes from the AOI, 20 map color ramps are grouped Light / Dark / Scientific (Dark Terrain group with elevation-anchored stops), industry presets (urban, agriculture, disaster, mining) pick the right product set in one click, STL/OBJ export prepares the terrain for 3D printing, and a run-history journal reopens past jobs.
